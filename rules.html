THEMESMP COMMUNITY

THEME SMP — RULES

Welcome to the official Theme SMP community!
Please read and follow the rules before participating in our Discord or Minecraft server.

01

Discord Rules

01

No Harassment

Harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination and targeted abuse are not allowed.

02

No NSFW Content

NSFW, sexual or excessively inappropriate content is strictly prohibited.

03

No Spam

Message spam, emoji spam, reaction spam, GIF flooding and unnecessary flooding are not allowed.

04

No Unnecessary Pings

Do not abuse @everyone, @here or staff/member role mentions.

05

No Unapproved Advertising

Advertising other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, communities, websites or services without staff permission is not allowed.

06

No Scams or Malicious Links

Phishing, scam links, malicious files, malware and suspicious links are strictly prohibited.

07

No Impersonation

Impersonating Theme SMP staff, creators or other community members is not allowed.

08

No Personal Information

Sharing another person's private information, passwords, private conversations or personal details without permission is prohibited.

09

No Unnecessary Drama

Creating or encouraging unnecessary public drama, targeted arguments or community conflicts is not allowed.

10

No Ban Evasion

Using alternate Discord accounts to bypass a punishment is prohibited.

11

No Staff Abuse

Threatening, harassing or intentionally disturbing staff members is not allowed.

12

No Malicious Content

Content intended to harm, exploit, scam or compromise another user or the Theme SMP community is prohibited.

02

Theme SMP — Lifesteal Rules

01

No Hacked Clients

Hacked clients, cheat clients and modifications that provide an unfair gameplay advantage are strictly prohibited.

02

No X-Ray

X-Ray, ore finders, cave finders and similar unfair resource-finding tools are not allowed.

03

No Auto Clickers / Macros

Auto clickers, combat macros and external automation that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited.

04

No Exploiting

Abusing bugs, glitches or unintended server mechanics for an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

05

No Duplication

Item, money, resource or any other form of duplication is not allowed.

06

No Combat Logging

Intentionally disconnecting during combat to avoid death or escape a fight is prohibited.

07

No Lag Machines

Creating machines, farms or builds specifically designed to cause excessive server lag is not allowed.

08

No Server Crashing

Attempting to crash, overload or intentionally damage the Minecraft server is strictly prohibited.

09

No Packet Abuse

Using packet manipulation, packet flooding or similar methods to gain an unfair advantage or affect server performance is prohibited.

10

No Ban Evasion

Using alternate Minecraft accounts to bypass a punishment is strictly prohibited.

11

No Unapproved Advertising

Advertising other Minecraft servers, Discord communities or services without permission is not allowed.

12

No Real-Money Scams

Scamming players through unofficial real-money transactions or falsely claiming to represent Theme SMP is prohibited.

13

No Staff Impersonation

Pretending to be a Theme SMP staff member or using a staff identity to deceive players is strictly prohibited.

14

No Threats

Real-life threats, doxxing threats or threats intended to cause genuine harm are strictly prohibited.

15

No Server Abuse

Any intentional attempt to damage, destabilize, overload or compromise Theme SMP is prohibited.

03

Important

These rules apply to all players and community members, regardless of rank or status.

Staff may take appropriate action against behaviour that harms the server or community, including situations that exploit loopholes in the rules.

Punishments may include warnings, mutes, temporary bans, permanent bans, resets or other appropriate actions depending on the severity of the violation.

Play fair. Protect your hearts. Welcome to Theme SMP.
